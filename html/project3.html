Demon Slayer Overview

Story Overview

Demon Slayer follows Tanjiro Kamado as he begins a dangerous journey after demons attack his family. His goal is to protect others and find a way to help his sister Nezuko.

Why the Series Is Popular

The series stands out because of its emotional storytelling, strong characters, and incredible animation. The battles are exciting, but the relationships between the characters are what make the story memorable.

Main Characters

  • Tanjiro Kamado
  • Nezuko Kamado
  • Zenitsu Agatsuma
  • Inosuke Hashibira
  • Kyojuro Rengoku
